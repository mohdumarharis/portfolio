Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

What's your typical project timeline? +

Project timelines vary depending on scope and complexity. A typical website project takes 4-8 weeks, while more complex applications may take 3-6 months. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

How do you handle project pricing? +

We offer both fixed-price and time-and-materials pricing models. After understanding your requirements, we'll provide a detailed quote with no hidden fees. We believe in transparent pricing throughout the entire process.

Do you provide ongoing support? +

Absolutely! We offer various support and maintenance packages to ensure your digital products remain secure, updated, and performing optimally. We're committed to long-term partnerships with our clients.

Can you work with our existing team? +

Yes! We frequently collaborate with in-house teams, other agencies, and freelancers. We're flexible and can adapt to your workflow and communication preferences.
